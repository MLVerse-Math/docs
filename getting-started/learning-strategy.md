# 🎯 Learning Strategy

Many learners struggle because they consume too much content without building projects.

MLVerse follows a practical approach.

---

## The 4-Step Framework

### Step 1: Learn

Understand the theory.

### Step 2: Visualize

Develop intuition using visualizations.

### Step 3: Implement

Build from scratch.

### Step 4: Apply

Use the concept in real-world projects.

---

## Recommended Ratio

20% Theory

30% Implementation

50% Projects

---

Theory

Mathematics
